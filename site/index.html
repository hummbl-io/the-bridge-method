01 / The method

A decision loop with a person at its center.

The Bridge Method is Reuben Bowlby’s public name for his documented coaching system. It helps a person and coach move from an intention to an observable task, a bounded decision, and a reviewable record.

Source of record

The current coaching canon was adopted on July 28, 2026. Its compact loop is:

govern → observe → prepare → train → adapt → record → review

  1. 01GovernName purpose, authority, and limits.
  2. 02ObserveSee the present without inventing certainty.
  3. 03PrepareSelect the smallest useful plan.
  4. 04TrainAct within the agreed guardrails.
  5. 05AdaptProceed, modify, defer, ask, or escalate.
  6. 06RecordKeep observation separate from interpretation.
  7. 07ReviewDecide together what the evidence changes.

The plan is a bridge.

The person still chooses to cross.

03 / Who it serves

Coaching for life outside the gym.

For the person

Your goal stays yours.

Training begins with why the task matters in your actual life. You retain informed choice, including the right to question, modify within authority, refuse, or stop.

For the coach

Your reasoning stays inspectable.

Purpose, observations, constraints, decisions, and changes remain distinct enough to review and improve together.

04 / The guardrails

A clear edge makes the bridge usable.

Coaching stays inside coaching. Missing information remains visible, and decisions outside scope go to the right authority.

The method supports

  • observable tasks and real-world context
  • shared decisions and bounded adjustments
  • the right to pause, refuse, or stop
  • notes that preserve what happened
  • clear escalation to qualified support

The method does not provide

  • diagnosis, treatment, or medical clearance
  • automatic exercise assignment
  • a universal readiness or flourishing score
  • surveillance or moral grades for adherence
  • guaranteed health or performance outcomes
